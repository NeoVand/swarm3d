# GPU contract (vgpu 0.5.0)

All distances use world units and velocities use units/second. Sphere positions have length R, plane positions have Y=0, cylinder positions have XZ radius R and bounded Y, and torus positions lie on a Y-axis ring with major radius R and tube radius r; all surface velocities are tangent. Config/species/rule storage rows are `vec4f` (16 bytes), with config row 14 holding raw integer words that shaders read through `bitcast<u32>` before any arithmetic. WGSL modules use vgpu's native import/export syntax.

`Particle`: position, velocity, previousVelocity (`vec4f`), identity (`vec4u`: stable id, species index, active=1, generation). 64 bytes. previousVelocity is transported to the new position during integration, so finite differences are covariant.

`Metrics`: 64 bytes, four `vec4f` rows: `(speed,turnRate,acceleration,neighborCount)`, `(density,anisotropy,polarization,radialFlow)`, `(headingAzimuth,centerDistance,centerBearing,flowOrbit)`, `(centerOrbitAngle,centerRadialSpeed,speedContrast,padding)`. IDs 0–14 follow this order; padding is zero in new measurements. IDs 8, 10, 11 and 12 are circular phases in [0,1).

`centerOrbitAngle` is unsigned center-relative velocity phase: project velocity and the outward mean-center direction onto the plane normal to the surface normal or configured volume orbit axis, then use `fract(atan2(dot(cross(v,outward),axis),dot(v,outward))/TAU+0.5)`. Phase zero is inward, one half is outward, and quarter-turn phases distinguish the two tangential senses. `centerRadialSpeed = dot(velocity,unit(meanDelta))` is signed units/s, positive inward. `speedContrast = abs(speed-length(meanTransportedNeighborVelocity))/sourceMaxSpeed`; the vector mean can cancel and is not the mean of neighbor speed magnitudes. These fields return zero for an empty neighborhood; orbit phase also returns zero for a vanishing projected direction, and radial speed for zero mean displacement. They participate in the same temporal filter as the other neighborhood estimates.

Surface obstacles are intrinsic disks serialized as sphere primitives; validation rejects surface boxes, although shaders defensively interpret a box row's bounding radius if directly supplied. Sphere interaction radii must be <πR/2; cylinder radii must be <πR. Plane radii have no curvature limit. Torus active interaction radii and complete obstacle influence reach must be strictly below 0.3r, with r≥1, 2≤R/r≤10 and R≤10000. Volume force disks/rings lie in the work plane with finite depth; surface forces use intrinsic distance and tangent directions. Plane periodicity affects X/Z only. Cylinder azimuth is periodic and its axial boundaries reflect; box/plane reflecting bounds and cylinder axial bounds inset by physical body radius. Exact half-period ties retain their original sign.

Visual channel blocks start at local rows 8 (hue),18(saturation),28(lightness). First row `(metricId or−1 constant, rangeMin,rangeMax,strength)`; next 8 rows are a 32-sample curve LUT. Last row `.x=enabled`. For enabled nonconstant channels, map source through range and LUT, then blend the base channel linearly toward LUT output with clamped strength [0,1], including hue. This appearance-strength blend is separate from circular metric filtering/differences. Species HSL bypasses the selected palette ramp unless hue has an enabled nonconstant mapping with positive strength. Rainbow uses ordinary HSL; other ramps apply saturation and brightness scaling after palette lookup. `agent_color` converts the resulting sRGB into linear RGB, shared by body rendering and history sampling.

Two metric-rule blocks start at 38 and 50. First row `(metricId,role:0 Neighbor/1 Self/2 Difference,behaviorId,strength)`; second row `(rangeMin,rangeMax,radius,enabled)`; next 8 rows are 32-sample curve LUT; final 2 rows reserved. Difference is absolute neighbor-self; circular differences use the shortest distance.

`pairRules` has one row per `(source,target)`, indexed `source*speciesCount+target`: `(behaviorId,strength,radius,enabled)`. CPU packing resolves explicit directed rule (including Ignore) before `from/'*'` fallback, independent of array order; null radius=source perception. Behavior IDs follow model BEHAVIORS: ignore0,flee1,chase2,cohere3,align4,orbit5,follow6,guard7,disperse8,mob9,mirror10,spiral11.

Align and Mirror use the actual transported neighbor velocity or its negative, capped at source maxSpeed, before subtracting source velocity and limiting force. Zero/slow targets stay zero/slow rather than being normalized into a full-speed goal. Spiral handedness is `+1` when `(sourceSpeciesIndex+targetSpeciesIndex)` is even and `−1` otherwise, around the local surface normal or volume axis.

Directed forces/counts accumulate separately for each target species. Ordinary behaviors average that bucket's participants, then apply their cap: 1×force for Align; 2× for Chase/Orbit; 1.5× for Cohere/Follow/Guard/Mirror; 3.5× for Mob; 3× for Spiral. Flee/Scatter sum urgency across threats before caps of 4×/5×force, respectively. Buckets are added before the shared total acceleration limit; unrelated species cannot dilute them. Each metric rule separately averages only its activated contributions. Disabled, Ignore, near-zero strength and curve activation at or below `1e−7` are excluded from their rule denominators.

Baseline flocking is same-species. Alignment/cohesion use cubic distance weights; separation radius is `max(2*physicalSize, perception*0.25)` and remains active during rebel windows. Rebels reduce alignment/cohesion by `1-clamp(strength,0,1)` with a small seeded exploration force; all-agent collision remains independent of directed Ignore rules. Size is a physical radius. Global collision radius is `(selfSize+otherSize)*config[5].y`. Maximum query radius must include all these radii. Speed, exact neighbor count, and nominal-neighborhood density are raw measurements; the other twelve fields use the stated exponential/circular smoothing alpha.

Contacts use an additional bounded Jacobi positional projection from the immutable current snapshot when distance is below `selfSize+otherSize`. The average half-overlap correction is bounded to `min(size*0.5,maxSpeed*dt*0.5)`; total intrinsic movement is capped to `maxSpeed*dt`. This is a contact constraint, separate from stored steering velocity: it neither injects a speed boost nor rewrites the prior metric snapshot. Surface correction uses the appropriate exponential map and transports current/prior velocities to the corrected tangent plane. Exact coincident directions are seeded, antisymmetric per stable-ID pair, finite and tangent on every surface.

Cruise propulsion is an explicit active behavior. First integrate the capped steering acceleration. If the candidate speed is below the species cruise target, use its heading (previous heading or a stable-ID/seed/generation tangent random heading at rest) and move the previous velocity toward that target by at most `maxForce*dt`, then cap at maxSpeed. Steering and cruise share the same total budget: stored covariant velocity acceleration remains bounded by maxForce for legal incoming speeds, while force0 cannot launch and speed can remain below cruise while accelerating. Boundary reflection and immediate clamping after reducing maxSpeed are external constraints and can exceed that derivative bound. Positional contact/obstacle corrections remain separate from stored velocity.

New species defaults pack maxSpeed 9.6 units/s, cruiseSpeed `0.6*maxSpeed` (5.76 units/s), and maxForce 14.4 units/s². The base scene's Amber species uses maxSpeed 10.8 and cruiseSpeed 6.48 with the same force limit. Missing prerelease cruise fields retain their separate `0.3*speed` migration value.

Simulation uses each source's local row6.x radius, with defensive lower bounds perception and2×self radius. CPU packing includes all-agent positional contacts, active soft collision range and effective nonzero directed/metric rule ranges. Metrics continue to measure only the observer species' perception neighborhood. No neighbor counts or cells are truncated.

## Grid and dispatch ordering

`grid` is 3*cellCount atomic u32: counts,exclusive offsets,scatter cursors. `indices` is population u32, `blocks` is ceil(cellCount/256) u32. Maximum 65536 cells. Entry shader grid.wgsl bindings: config0,particles1,grid2,indices3,blocks4. Entry points: clear_grid, count_particles, prefix_cells, prefix_blocks, finish_prefix, scatter_particles. Workgroup 256; prefix_cells dispatch ceil(cells/256), prefix_blocks dispatch 1; clear_grid/finish_prefix dispatch ceil(cells/256); count/scatter dispatch ceil(population/256). Every active particle occupies one complete prefix/scatter slot, no truncation.

Build grid for current particles before simulation. Simulation bindings: config0,current1,next2,grid3,indices4,metrics5,species6,pairRules7. `simulate` workgroup 128. After swap, rebuild grid for newly integrated positions before measuring. `measure` bindings config0,particles1,previousMetrics2,nextMetrics3,grid4,indices5,species6; workgroup 128. Swap metrics only after measurement. New-run bootstrap uses alpha1. Reconciliation/query refresh uses alpha0 to refresh speed/count/density while retaining survivor filters; a prior metric speed of -1 marks a newborn and initializes all fifteen fields regardless of alpha. Never read/write the same particle or metric buffer in one dispatch.

The runtime compiles a bounded set of native compute pipelines through the public device handle, retaining vgpu-owned core buffers. It caches bind groups for both particle/metric sides and rebinds on structural replacement. Each coherent tick has one config write and one compute submission. All passes within that tick use the same immutable configuration snapshot. Several ticks remain separate submissions so later config writes cannot overwrite earlier tick values. For a single prefix block, the block-prefix and aggregation dispatches are omitted.

History bindings config0,particles1,history2,metrics3,species4; `write_history` workgroup 128. History allocates `capacity*64*32` bytes in SOA form: a position/generation prefix and a linear RGB/valid suffix. For `slot=particleIndex*samples+head%samples`, `history[slot]=vec4(position.xyz,generation)` and `history[u32(config[15].z)+slot]=vec4(agent_color(...),1)`. Config15.z is `capacity*64` vec4 entries, never population*64 for a larger capacity. History binds the newly completed particle and metric sides, so color and position refer to the same observation.

CPU initialization fills newborn position slots at spawn and leaves older color validity zero. Initial/reconciliation bootstrap measures first, then writes the current head's position/color. A sampled physical tick advances the ring head and writes both records; a nonsampled tick leaves them unchanged. The runtime supplies the explicit write-request flag at its sampling boundary. Trail age uses elapsed simulation time since the actual newest sample, independently of raw tick modulus. Structural migration remaps both SOA blocks by stable ID, anchors positions at now, and resamples older positions/colors by physical age. Older stored colors interpolate in linear RGB/valid; ages within the unsampled gap hold the old newest color until the reanchored head is refreshed. Generation tokens are never interpolated across discontinuities. Invalid/nonfinite color records fall back to the current computed color. Render skips zero-length or discontinuous position segments.

## Rendering

Camera uniform: `viewProjection:mat4x4f`, `position:vec4f`, `right:vec4f`, `up:vec4f` (112 bytes). `boids.wgsl`: config0,particles1,metrics2,species3, camera uniform4; vs_main/fs_main. Draw 36 vertices, population instances; opaque depth test/write.

`trails.wgsl`: config0,particles1,metrics2,species3,history4,camera uniform5; vs_main/fs_main. Draw 6*activeSegments vertices, population instances; premultiplied alpha, depth test, no depth writes. Active segments are bounded by valid history and the longest visible species trail; the draw is omitted when all trails are disabled. Segment0 attaches the current particle position/appearance to the newest stored position/color; subsequent segments use their recorded endpoint colors. RGB interpolates linearly along each segment. Stored valid colors survive later palette/HSL/curve changes, while width, opacity and physical-age fading use current trail settings. The fragment shader adds a colored luminous center with soft shoulders in the same ribbon pass.

The source-defined `scripts/gpu-rendering.mjs` gate checks known linear RGB/valid output, history retention/interpolation, capacity greater than population, all fifteen metric appearance sources, scalar hue-strength continuity, five lit body shapes and depth-only near/far occlusion without background coloring. Review PNGs are saved to `.cache/rendering/`; these described checks are not execution results.

Trail validity depends on recorded generation, finite nonzero endpoints/segments and world geometry, independently of current maxSpeed or body size. Periodic box/plane segments that cross an active axis seam (raw endpoint difference greater than that axis half-extent) are skipped rather than drawn through the box. Reflecting volume paths retain long straight segments after paused parameter edits. Sphere/cylinder segments skip zero radial endpoints and near-antipodal angular pairs whose shorter interpolation arc is ambiguous. Torus segments cross both chart seams continuously and skip near-half-period angular ambiguities. Surface endpoint widths are projected and lifted on their own manifold. Six-vertex segments approximate intermediate curved motion with rasterized chords.

`world.wgsl`: config0,camera uniform1; vs_main/fs_main. Explicit sparse guides draw box boundary lines, sphere latitude/longitude lines, bounded plane grid, cylinder axial/ring grid or torus chart grid: 72 box, 4464 sphere, 132 plane, 2928 cylinder or 4608 torus vertices. `showBoundary` defaults off and omits this pass; enabled guides use depth test, no depth write, alpha and derivative-based edge coverage. No world or trail pass substitutes for real 3D depth-tested body geometry.

Additional world entries share the same two bindings: `vs_shell/fs_shell` draws 6 vertices for the plane shell and 10800 for sphere/cylinder shells or 55296 for the torus shell (depth write, color write mask disabled). This depth-only surface pass hides far-side agents without painting a shell or changing the background, independently of optional guides. `vs_obstacles/fs_obstacles` draws 10800 vertices with obstacleCount instances (opaque matching volume spheres/boxes or intrinsic surface disks, depth write); `vs_force/fs_force` draws 1536 vertices (outer radius and ring peak/disk half-radius contours, premultiplied alpha, no depth write). All geometry uses the real world-space collision/force radius. The force contour lies on the actual work plane or surface and follows the cursor active flag. Periodic plane disks/contours use four copies, clipped to the physical bounds and each copy’s minimum-image chart, so large radii do not draw false contours from a farther periodic image. Cylinder disks/contours clip at the axial boundaries. Torus disks/contours use the inverse of the same local midpoint log as their distance classifier.

`vs_plane/fs_plane` draws 492 vertices (41 lines on each workplane axis), clipped to the actual volume box, premultiplied alpha and depth test without depth writing. It is enabled for volume Force and Obstacle tools even without a cursor hit. Palette colors are converted from sRGB to linear before lighting; presentation owns exposure and transfer encoding.

Bodies normalize geometric normals after interpolation, with a rounded normal for bead bodies and front/back facing correction. Linear body color receives directional key/fill diffuse light, a colored rim and a hue-preserving specular highlight; selection adds a separate accent. History stores the unlit linear appearance color, rather than lighting tied to a previous camera. Trail core/shoulder shading and final HDR exposure/bloom operate on that linear color.

For the torus, the grid and disk/force overlays lift by at least 0.004r and histories by at least 0.0035r to remain visible over the concave mesh facets; these presentation offsets change no stored physical positions.

All stage draws share one public `uniforms` camera block, updated once per rendered frame. Fast caps the HDR stage at one pixel per CSS pixel, Balanced at 1.25, and Sharp uses native device pixels up to 2. The final canvas remains at native resolution up to 2 in all three modes. Optional bloom extracts highlights into a quarter-width/height target with 4 samples, filters that target with 9 samples, then composites one glow sample in `presentation.wgsl`. Bloom-off frames omit the two glow passes. Rendering quality changes no particle population, query radius, timestep, or physical rule.

## Torus approximation and native gate

The ring torus uses the induced chart metric `r² dθ² + (R+r cosθ)² dφ²`. Local displacement/distance use the symmetric midpoint estimate from `src/lib/model/torus.ts`, with the midpoint vector transported back by the exact FIRST-HALF connection integral. Neighbor vectors use exact Levi-Civita transport along the chosen short straight chart path. That path generally differs from a geodesic; neither the midpoint log nor its distance is claimed to be an exact torus geodesic. The bearing frame is continuous through both seams: east=−ePhi, north=eTheta, outward normal=eTheta×ePhi.

Embedded cell queries use the conservative padding `q*(1+q/(2*(R-r)))`, proved for the midpoint classifier. Complete prefix/scatter membership is unchanged; there is no neighbor/cell cap. Density remains count divided by nominal flat local disk area `πq²`, without curvature or boundary correction. Counts are complete for this chosen approximate classifier, not a claim of exact true-geodesic threshold membership.

Physical motion uses second-order midpoint connection integration, subdividing intrinsic travel into steps no longer than 0.05r. Each internal connection solve uses at most eight Float32 fixed-point iterations and a 1e−7-radian stopping threshold. Candidate and previous velocities receive the same accumulated rotation along the actual motion path. Obstacle projection follows a separate chart path after that integration. Classifier-consistent disk projection/rendering uses a distinct eight-iteration inverse midpoint-log helper. History endpoints remain the actual recorded world positions; ribbons remain sampled piecewise linear geometry.

`scripts/gpu-torus.mjs` defines packed native Float32 relation, transport, motion, normal and inverse-offset gates over R/r={2,3,5,10}, r={1,10,1000}, including R=10000 and both chart seams, plus an independent local shooting subset. In the historical native Apple M4 run before the current metric/history/rendering updates, observed maxima versus the CPU midpoint model were: distance 4.35e−6r, displacement 4.65e−6r, transport 2.59e−7r, motion 5.55e−6r, inverse offset 2.31e−6r. Shooting comparisons observed maximum distance error 0.1031%, log-direction error 0.001913 rad and connection error 0.000606 rad. These are sampled historical observations, not uniform error theorems or fresh results for the current revision. The asserted shooting gate is distance<0.17%, direction<0.004 rad, connection<0.001 rad; Float32 spatial gates are ≤1.2e−5r depending on the field.

The same script checks exact neighbor counts and all fifteen CPU-reference measurements for uniform/dense/both-seam/tube-top cases; free paths at ratios 2 and 10 against RK4 with speed/tangency and zero covariant derivatives; all twelve directed behavior semantics; same/cross-species coincident contact recovery; long per-source queries; obstacle/force behavior; and depth-tested concave inner-ring bodies, short seam-crossing trails, force contours and obstacle disks. Box, sphere, plane, cylinder and batching regression suites remain separate gates. These are the source-defined checks, not a claim of a fresh successful run after contract changes.
